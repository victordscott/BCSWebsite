About the BCS Team

Thought leadership, put to work.

We are a San Antonio, Texas–based, minority-owned team that designs and implements practical ideas to improve how organizations operate — for federal programs, for prime contractors, and for the neighborhoods we call home.

Our story

From management consulting to secure federal delivery.

Bright Consulting Solutions began as a management consulting firm focused on program management, strategic planning, enterprise risk, and information technology. Our leadership team brings more than 60 years of corporate, small business, and government contracting experience. Registered in SAM.gov since 2011, we grew through repeat business as a subcontractor on NASA, DHS, FEMA, and City of San Antonio programs.

As federal requirements for protecting Controlled Unclassified Information tightened, we built our own Microsoft government cloud architecture — with a GCC High tenant as the documented CUI boundary — and turned that experience into cyber and cloud services for others.

Alongside that work, we founded Bright Community Solutions, Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity, to bring the same discipline to health equity, food security, workforce, and civic engagement in San Antonio. Today the two entities operate as The BCS Team: one mission, two engines.
