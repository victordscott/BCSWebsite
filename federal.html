Dual-entity governed boundary

Shared leadership. Separate systems.

The BCS Team operates as two legally and operationally separate entities. Shared leadership is disclosed, and identity, access, technology, financial, and data-handling controls preserve both boundaries.

  • Distinct financial controls and separate books
  • Segregated technology tenants and identities
  • CUI handling restricted to the Bright Consulting Solutions LLC environment
  • Related-party roles managed under documented conflict-of-interest procedures
